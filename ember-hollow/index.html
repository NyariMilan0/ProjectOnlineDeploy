WELCOME TO THE HOLLOW

Small sparks add up.

Click the fire, use the gold button, or press Space to collect embers. You begin with manual clicks only. Upgrade your bellows to discover new tools, then bind an Ember sprite to unlock automatic production.

Dry kindling strengthens clicks. A Stone hearth, Charcoal bed, and Chimney draft improve the fire as more of the workshop is revealed. Later, discover Blue spark for 100,000 embers in the Blue flame workshop. Each manual click then has a 1% chance to find a blue flame. The Cobalt lens improves that chance, and Azure resonance multiplies the amount found. Both use blue flames.

The Blue siphon uses embers to make automatic blue-flame rolls, including while you are away. A roll uses your current chance and drop amount. As you invest, more blue upgrades appear: Azure embers boosts all ember production, Twin flicker can double a drop, Blue wisp speeds up the siphon, and Spark scatter adds extra rolls to every manual click.

Rebirth begins at 1,000 blue flames for one point. Every additional point costs twice the previous point, across all lives. Multiple points add their costs together.

After your first rebirth, permanent skills can use rebirth points and blue-flame fees. Warm memory needs just your first point. Most skills have five ranks; starting gifts apply on your next rebirth.

Rebirth resets this life's resources and upgrades. Permanent skills, points, achievements, and lifetime records remain. Full reset in Settings clears everything.

The soundtrack begins after your first interaction. Music, volume, and interaction sounds have separate controls in Settings. Achievement celebrations respect your reduced-motion setting.

After your second rebirth the Ash Grove opens: trees produce ash (it never resets), ash buys boosts for the fire and helpers that buy upgrades and can rebirth for you.

Use the ×1 / ×10 / MAX switch above the workshop to buy several levels at once. In Settings you can export your save as a code or file and import it elsewhere.

Progress saves every 2 seconds and when leaving. Base offline progress covers up to 8 hours, with longer dreams available through skills.
